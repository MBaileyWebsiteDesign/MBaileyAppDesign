Questions people ask

Straight answers about web apps, PWAs and working with M Bailey App Design.

Will my app work on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac?

Yes. A web app runs in the browser, so one app works on Android and iPhone phones, iPads and other tablets, and Windows and Mac computers, using current versions of the major browsers. I design and test the layout for each screen size.

Do people need to download it from an app store?

No. People open a web app with a web address, so you can share it by link, email or QR code. If it’s built as a Progressive Web App, people on supported devices can also add it to their home screen or desktop.

Can you put my app in the App Store or Google Play?

A web app isn’t listed in the app stores by default. If a store listing matters to your business, we’ll talk about whether a native app or a packaged version is worth the extra cost, review processes and maintenance.

Will it work offline?

Only if offline support is designed in, and only for the parts where it makes sense. A PWA can keep certain screens and data available without a connection, but apps that share live information between users usually need a connection for most tasks. We’ll decide together what should work offline.

Can it send push notifications?

It can on many devices, if the app is built for it and the user gives permission. On iPhone and iPad, web notifications only work once the app has been added to the Home Screen. Email notifications are a reliable option on every device.

Can a web app use the camera, location or Bluetooth?

The camera, microphone and location are widely available to web apps, with the user’s permission. Bluetooth, NFC and USB access is limited and not available in Safari on iPhone and iPad. If your app depends on hardware like this, a native app may be the better choice, and I’ll tell you that at the start.

How secure will my app be?

Security is built in from the start: encrypted HTTPS connections, secure sign-in, role-based permissions, careful handling of personal data and regular dependency updates. Where the app holds personal data, it’s designed with UK GDPR in mind.

How much does a web app cost?

It depends on what the app needs to do. A focused tool for one team is a very different project from a public-facing product with payments and integrations. After an initial conversation, you’ll get a written scope and an individual quote before any work starts.

How long does it take?

That depends on the scope too. Your quote will include a realistic timeline with stages, so you’ll see working previews along the way instead of waiting for one big reveal.

Who owns the app and the data?

Ownership is set out clearly in your agreement before the project starts. Your data is yours, and I can provide exports of it.

What happens after launch?

I offer ongoing support covering hosting, security and software updates, fixes and new features. Apps work best when they keep improving based on how people actually use them.

For websites, my sister studio M Bailey Website Design is the right place. If you need both a website and an app, they can be planned together.
