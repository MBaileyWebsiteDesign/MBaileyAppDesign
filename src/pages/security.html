Security, data and quality

Your app will hold information that matters to you and your users. Here’s how it’s looked after, from the first design to long after launch.

Encrypted connections

Every app is served over HTTPS, so information travelling between the browser and the server is encrypted.

Secure sign-in

Passwords are never stored in readable form. Sign-in is protected against repeated guessing, and stronger options such as sign-in links or two-step verification can be added where needed.

The right access for each person

Roles and permissions are designed in from the start, so each person only sees and changes what they should.

Personal data and UK GDPR

Apps are designed to collect only what’s needed, keep it only as long as it’s needed, and make it straightforward to answer requests from the people it belongs to.

Hosting and backups

Apps run on established cloud platforms, in UK or European regions where the service offers them, with backups so your data can be recovered.

Updates and monitoring

Software dependencies are kept up to date and security fixes applied as part of ongoing support. Errors are logged so problems can be spotted and fixed.

Quality you can rely on

Security is one part of a well-built app. These standards apply to every project.

  • Accessible by designBuilt to WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines: keyboard use, screen readers, readable contrast and clear forms.
  • Tested on real devicesChecked in current browsers on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Mac before launch.
  • Fast to loadLean code and sensible caching, so the app feels quick even on a mobile connection.
  • Your data is yoursOwnership is agreed in writing before the project starts, and your data can be exported.
  • DocumentedPlain-English notes on how the app works and how to manage it day to day.
  • SupportedOngoing support keeps the app secure, updated and improving after launch.
