How a project works

A clear, step-by-step process with you involved throughout. You see working software early and often, not just at the end.

  1. An initial conversation

    Tell me about your idea or the problem you want to solve. We’ll talk about who will use the app, what they need to do and which devices they use. I’ll give you an honest view on whether a web app, a PWA, a native app or an existing product suits you best.

  2. Scope and quote

    I write up what the app will do, the main screens, the users and their permissions, any integrations, and what’s left for later. You get a quote based on that scope, so we both know what’s being built.

  3. Design

    I design the key screens and journeys for mobile and desktop. You can click through them, share them with colleagues and ask for changes before development begins.

  4. Build in stages

    Development happens in stages, each ending with a working preview at a private web address. You try it on your own phone and computer and tell me what you think.

  5. Test on real devices

    Before launch the app is tested across current browsers on Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Mac, along with checks on accessibility, security and performance.

  6. Launch

    The app goes live on your web address. If it’s a PWA, it’s also ready to install on supported devices. I help you bring in your first users and data.

  7. Support and improve

    Once it’s live, I keep it updated and secure and keep developing it based on real feedback.

What helps at the start

You don’t need a technical specification. It helps to have:

  • A rough description of who will use the app and what for.
  • Any spreadsheets, forms or tools you use today.
  • Examples of apps you like or dislike, and why.
  • Any deadlines, such as the start of a season or a product launch.
