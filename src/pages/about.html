A specialist studio for web applications

M Bailey App Design is run by Matt Bailey. You work with the person designing and building your app, from the first conversation onwards.

Why a separate studio for apps

Building an application is different from building a website. It involves user accounts, data, business rules, security and long-term support. M Bailey App Design focuses on that work so it gets the attention it deserves.

It sits alongside two sister studios. M Bailey Website Design builds websites, and M Bailey Photos handles photography. If your project needs a marketing website or photography as well as an app, it can all be coordinated in one place.

How I work

  • Direct. You talk to the person doing the work, with no account managers in between.
  • Honest. If a web app isn’t the right answer, or a feature isn’t worth the cost, I’ll say so.
  • Clear. Plain English, written scopes and regular previews, so you always know where things stand.
  • In it for the long run. Apps need looking after. I’m here to support and improve yours after launch.

What I believe about software

Good software should feel simple to the people using it, even when what it does is complicated. It should work on whatever device they have to hand. For most business, club and community apps, the open web does that well.
