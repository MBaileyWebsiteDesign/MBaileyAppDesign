Web apps for the way you work

The same approach suits very different groups: one app at one web address, built around how your people actually work. Here are the kinds of app each group usually asks for.

Clubs and leagues

For sports clubs, leagues and societies run by committees and volunteers.

Club admin often lives in spreadsheets, group chats and paper forms, all kept going by a few busy volunteers. A club app puts it in one place that members can open on their phone and committee members can manage from a laptop.

  • Court, pitch and room bookings
  • Membership sign-up and renewals
  • Fixtures, results and league tables
  • Team selection and availability
  • Subscriptions and match fees
  • Notices for members

Small and growing businesses

For teams that have outgrown spreadsheets or can’t find software that fits.

Off-the-shelf software makes you work its way. A bespoke tool follows your process: the steps your jobs go through, the information you need on site, and the reports you actually look at.

  • Job sheets and scheduling
  • Quotes and approvals
  • Customer portals
  • Stock and equipment tracking
  • Staff rotas and timesheets
  • Management dashboards

Charities, schools and organisations

For groups that handle records, people and personal data.

Organisations need secure access, clear permissions and careful handling of personal information. A web app gives staff and volunteers one reliable place to record and find information, on whatever device they have.

  • Volunteer and staff rotas
  • Case and contact records
  • Online forms and applications
  • Secure document sharing
  • Event and course bookings
  • Role-based access

Entrepreneurs and new products

For people with an idea they want to test with real users.

The web is a quick route from idea to something people can use. Share a link and your first users are in, with no app store review. Start with the features that prove the idea, then build on what you learn.

  • A focused first version
  • Sign-up and user accounts
  • Subscriptions and payments
  • Admin tools to run the service
  • Usage reporting
  • Installable PWA

Individuals

For personal projects, hobbies and specialist tools.

Sometimes the tool you need doesn’t exist, or the ones that do are too complicated. A small web app can do one job well, for you or for a group you’re part of.

  • Personal trackers and logs
  • Collection catalogues
  • Training and coaching tools
  • Group organisers
