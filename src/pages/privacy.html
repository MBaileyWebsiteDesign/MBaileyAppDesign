Privacy notice

How this website handles your information. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by M Bailey App Design. For any privacy question, email matt@mbaileyappdesign.co.uk.

What we collect

If you send an enquiry using the contact form, we collect your name, email address, any organisation name, the type of enquirer you choose and your message. If you email us directly, we receive whatever you include in the email.

The web server keeps short-term technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. This site doesn’t use advertising or analytics cookies.

Why we use it

We use your enquiry details to reply to you and, if you go ahead, to discuss and deliver your project. Our lawful basis is your consent when you submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to a project are deleted within 12 months. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as needed for the work and for our legal and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. It’s processed by the services that run this site and our email, including our hosting provider and email provider, only so they can provide those services.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us to make a request. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Installing this site

This website can be installed as a Progressive Web App on supported devices. To do that, your browser stores copies of some pages and files on your device. They contain no personal information, and you can remove them by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.
