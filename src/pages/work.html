League management

In use

Website builders don’t include proper competition management, so leagues were running on spreadsheets. This app gives organisers, captains and players one place for the whole season.

  • One sign-in for admins, captains and players, with permissions for each role
  • Singles, team and doubles divisions
  • Round-robin leagues and single or double-elimination knockouts
  • A five-step wizard to set up a whole new season, including importing players from a spreadsheet
  • Frame-by-frame results, live standings, player profiles and a trophy cabinet
  • Public links for brackets, tables and fixtures, and an overlay for live streams
  • Tools for venue managers and bar staff, check-in, membership and an audit log
  • React
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • Fly.io

Virtual Training

Early build

A 3D English pool simulator that runs in the browser, built with training at its core.

  • A 3D table with realistic ball physics, including spin, throw and cushion rebounds
  • Eight scored training exercises with feedback and medals
  • Daily challenges, a ladder and timed rounds
  • Two-player matches under WPA Blackball rules, including on two phones
  • Aim by touch, mouse or keyboard, with optional shot guides

  • Three.js
  • TypeScript
  • Fly.io

AI technique coaching

Proof of concept

Upload a short clip of yourself playing and get coaching feedback back on the same page.

  • Accepts video clips of up to a minute and checks them before processing
  • Samples frames from the clip and analyses them with an AI vision model
  • Feedback on stance, bridge hand, grip, cue alignment and follow-through
  • Each point comes with a suggestion and a confidence level
  • If the AI doesn’t give a usable answer, it says so instead of making one up
  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • FFmpeg
  • Claude AI
  • Fly.io

Family game, installable

Dragons and Tomatoes

A fantasy take on Snakes and Ladders for two or three players. Play on one shared device, or each player on their own phone with a four-letter game code.

  • Installable on phones and tablets. One-device games work offline
  • Multi-device games where the server rolls the dice and checks every turn
  • A host can roll for a player whose device drops out
  • Winners earn colours for their dragon. Collect seven to become a Rainbow Dragon
  • Board balance tested by simulating 20,000 games
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Node.js
  • PWA
  • Fly.io

Learning platform concept

PlayTinker

A concept for a free place where young people play browser games, create their own with AI as a helper, and learn real software skills. The child is the creator and AI is the tool.

  • Playable games, each with Inspect to see the real code and Remix to change settings live
  • A working Prompt Builder and Creator’s Check that explain good prompting
  • A seven-level academy with progress points
  • Guidance pages for parents and educators, plus privacy and safety commitments
  • Separate test and live sites, with automatic deployment
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • Automated tests
  • Fly.io

Business website with automation

NVQ Pro

A training provider’s website with the business processes built in, not just pages. It’s built on Wix Headless, so it uses Wix’s CMS and store with a custom front end.

  • Records of learners who have bought or submitted an NVQ, and of people registering interest
  • Public forms that write securely into the records, which only admins can read
  • Automatic reminder emails a month before a learner’s qualification expires
  • Online store and invoicing
